## Sections
Settings › Sections lists every place you keep things, in the order you walk the truck. Name them the way your crew talks: "Lowboy", "Dry shelf 2", "Cab". Each section shows how many products it holds.
- **Add section**: type a name, or tap a food-truck idea to add it.
- **Move up / Move down**: changes the walking order. Count sheets and voice counts follow this order.
- **Rename**: type over the name and save.
- **Delete section**: if it still holds products, pick where to move them, then **Move & save**.

## Zones
Every product also has a zone, the broad type of storage: Cooler, Freezer, Dry storage or Prep. Set it on the product in Product master. Zones drive the storage map and the zone badges you see on lists.

## The storage map
On the truck › Zones shows what is on the truck right now, zone by zone, using your latest counts. Use it to answer "do we have any left?" without walking the truck.
