## The product master
Inventory sheets › Product master lists every product with its unit, PAR, section, cost and vendor. Search at the top to find one fast.

## The fields
- **Name**, and an optional **Spanish name** and **Other names** (aliases) so voice counts match what your crew says.
- **Unit**: how you count it (case, lb, ea, bag, can, qt, gal, pack, box, tub, bottle).
- **Pack / Case size**: how many come in a case.
- **PAR / min**: how much you want on hand. Below PAR shows on Home and on the Order list.
- **Cost per unit**: what you pay per counted unit. Receiving an invoice updates it to the latest price.
- **Category**, **Section/Zone**, **Vendor**, **SKU / item #** and **Barcode**.

## Adding products
- **Add item** for one product.
- **Starter list** (Inventory sheets › Starter list): a few hundred common food-truck products. Tick what you carry and press **Add selected**. Prices and PARs start blank: type in what you actually pay.
- **Import CSV**: upload or paste a CSV with the columns name, unit, par, cost, category, zone, vendor, sku, barcode. Matching names are updated, new names are added. **Download template** gives you the header row.
- **Scan barcode** fills the barcode field (Chrome on Android or desktop; type the code anywhere else).

## Editing and archiving
Press **Edit** on a row to change it. **Archive** hides a product without deleting its count history, so old reports stay right.

## Export
**Export CSV** downloads your product list for a spreadsheet or your bookkeeper.
