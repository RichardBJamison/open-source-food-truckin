The Problem

Every truck operator knows the drill.

End of the night. Generator still running. Count the cooler by flashlight. Guess the freezer. Scribble numbers on a ticket pad. Walk to the laptop in the cab. Type it into a spreadsheet someone made in 2019. Try to reconcile against last night’s Square or Toast export. Watch it never add up. Text the prep cook tomorrow’s load list. Repeat.

Brick-and-mortar restaurants have been sold $249-a-month inventory platforms and invoice scanners built for four walls. Trucks get the leftover: a clipboard, a group chat, and a hope that the ice cream doesn’t thaw before Saturday’s festival.

AI changed everything. Now we can build this program for free. So we did.

The Workshop

Precision instruments for the rolling craft.

One free program. The full workflow lives inside it — Home base, inventory sheets, reports, on the truck, all inputs, menu costing, stops and load lists.

01

Smart inventory sheets

Count sheet, variance, order list, and product master — cooler, freezer, dry, prep — with PAR, pack size, vendor, and Spanish names.

02

Variance vs. POS sales

What you should have used from Square, Toast, Clover, or any CSV, against what the counts say you used.

03

Optional invoice photos

Type invoice lines yourself — or connect your own AI key to read paper invoice photos and PDFs. Off by default.

04

380-product starter list

Common food-truck products with units and pack sizes. No prices stuffed in — you enter what you actually pay.
