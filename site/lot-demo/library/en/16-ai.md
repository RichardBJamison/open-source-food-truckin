## You don't need AI
The program works fully without it. AI is off until you switch it on, and nothing is ever sent while it is off.

## What AI does here
- Reads an invoice photo or PDF into vendor, date and lines you check before receiving.
- Looks at a shelf photo and suggests the products and counts for that section.
- Tidies a messy spoken walk into a clean product list.
- Turns Help into a tutor that explains steps in plain words, using this Library and your truck's numbers.

AI only suggests. Nothing is saved until you confirm it.

## Set it up
1. Settings › AI. Pick the **AI service**: ChatGPT (OpenAI), Claude (Anthropic), Grok (xAI), Local (Ollama, no key) or Other (OpenAI-compatible).
2. Paste your **API key** from that company's website. It is stored on this computer only, inside the program's data folder. It is never shown again and is left out of backups.
3. Press **Test connection**. You should see "Connected".
4. Turn on **Use AI**.

## Which model
For invoice and shelf photos the model must read images (for example gpt-4o-mini, a Claude Sonnet model, grok-4 or llama3.2-vision). Help works with any chat model.

## Cost and privacy
Your AI company bills you directly, usually a fraction of a cent per question and a little more per photo. Files are sent only when you press an AI button. Help sends your question, the Library pages it found and a short summary of your truck (product, count and order numbers), never your PINs or keys.

## Turn it off
Switch **Use AI** off, or press **Remove key**. You can also revoke the key on the AI company's website.
