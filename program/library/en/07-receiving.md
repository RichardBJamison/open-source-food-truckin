## Log a delivery
All inputs › Invoices & receiving. Pick the product, enter the quantity, cost per unit and vendor, then **Log receive**. On-hand goes up and the product's cost updates to what you just paid.

## Attach the invoice
Add a photo or PDF of the invoice to keep it with the receive (**Choose file(s)** or **Take photo**). It is stored in the program's data folder.

## Read an invoice with AI (optional)
With AI on, press **Read with AI**. It reads vendor, invoice number, date and lines. Check the invoice: match each line to a product or add it as new. If a match looks shaky it says "Not sure: check this match". Nothing is saved until you press **Save to receiving & costs**.

With AI off, attach the file for your records and type the lines.

## Many lines at once
All inputs › Invoice takes one line per product: name, quantity, price. Paste the lines, press **Read lines**, check the matches and **Receive all**. Unmatched lines are skipped, so add new products first.

## Costs
Cost per unit is the last price you received at (not an average). Plate costs and food cost % use it.
