## The order list
Inventory sheets › Order list shows everything under PAR, grouped by the vendor you last bought it from, with the amount to order and an estimated total. **Print** it for a shopping run, or export it.

## Purchase orders
Inventory sheets › Orders. Press **Make orders** to build one order per vendor from the order list. Each order is a Draft until you send it.
- **Copy** or **Open email draft** puts the order in your mail app. The program never sends email itself.
- **Mark sent** when it's gone.
- **Receive** when it arrives: change any amount that came in different, then **Confirm received**. On-hand and costs update.

## Vendors
Settings › Vendors holds who you buy from: name, rep, phone, email, account #, order cutoff, delivery days, lead days, terms, how to order and notes.
