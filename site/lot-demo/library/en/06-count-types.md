## The three types
Settings › Count types:
- **Daily end-of-shift**: a partial count of a watch list. Pick sections, categories or individual products.
- **Weekly**: the full truck.
- **Monthly in-depth**: the full truck plus an extras checklist.

## Settings for each type
- **Full count (whole truck)** on or off.
- **Sections in this count**, **Categories in this count** and **Watch-list products** for partial counts.
- **Leave these sections out**: for example, leave the commissary out of the weekly.
- **When**: Before service or End of shift (after today's sales).
- **Every (days)**: a reminder label only; nothing is scheduled automatically.

## Who can start which count
In Settings › People, each person has **Count types this person can start**. Leave all unticked to allow every type. Tick only Daily to keep crew on end-of-shift counts.

## How partial counts affect reports
Partial counts only answer for what they cover. The weekly sales report runs between your two latest full counts and skips the daily partials in between. A product counted only on a daily list uses its own last count.
