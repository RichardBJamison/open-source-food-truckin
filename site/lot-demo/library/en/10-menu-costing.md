## Add a plate
Menu & costing › **Add menu item**. Enter the name, the menu price, and the name or names it has in your POS (comma separated).

## Ingredients
Press **+ Ingredient** for each product in the plate and enter the quantity per plate **in that product's own unit**. If tortillas are counted in packs of 30, one tortilla is 0.033 pack. If beef is counted by the pound, a quarter pound is 0.25.

The program doesn't convert units yet. A product bought by the case but used by the pound shows false variance until the units match.

## What you get
- **Cost / plate**: the sum of each ingredient times its cost per unit.
- **Food cost %**: cost per plate divided by menu price.
- **Margin**: menu price minus cost per plate.

If an ingredient has no cost yet, the plate says so. Receive an invoice or type the cost in Product master.
