## Business
Settings › Business holds the truck or business name, legal name, address, phone, email, website, logo (under 300 KB), time zone, days between counts and the day your week starts. The name and logo show on orders and reports. Units (imperial or metric) and language are set here too.

## Categories and GL codes
Settings › Accounts › Categories group products for cost reports. Each category can have a GL code for your bookkeeper (optional) and a target food cost %. Reports compare your real food cost to the target.

## POS accounts
Also under Accounts: which register accounts feed sales in. They label imports.
