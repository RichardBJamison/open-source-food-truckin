## PAR
How much of a product you want on hand. Below PAR means it goes on the order list.

## On hand
What your latest count says is on the truck, plus anything received since.

## Section and zone
A section is a named place you store things (Lowboy, Dry shelf 2). A zone is the broad type of storage (Cooler, Freezer, Dry storage, Prep).

## Full count and partial count
A full count covers the whole truck. A partial (daily) count covers a watch list and leaves everything else at its last count.

## Variance
The difference between what you should have and what you counted, or between what sales explain and what you used.

## Theoretical usage
Quantity sold × recipe quantity, added up for every plate. What you would have used with perfect portions and no waste.

## Food cost %
Cost of the food in a plate (or a period) divided by what it sold for.

## Stop type and load list
A kind of stop (Lunch, Brewery, Festival) with its own PARs, and the printable list of what to load for it.

## PO
Purchase order: an order to one vendor built from the order list.

## GL code
General ledger code: the account number your bookkeeper uses for a category. Optional.

## EXAMPLE
Demo data from the sample taco truck. Not real numbers.
