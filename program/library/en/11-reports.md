## Variance (counts)
Inventory sheets › Variance compares your last completed count to PAR and to the count before it.

## Sales vs counts
Reports › Sales vs counts shows what sales say you used against what your counts show, in dollars, between your two latest full counts:
- **Counted usage** = start count + received − end count.
- **Theoretical** = for every plate sold, quantity sold × recipe quantity.
- **Variance** = counted − theoretical. Positive means more was used than sales explain: waste, over-portioning, comps, theft or a delivery you forgot to receive.

Daily partial counts in between are skipped for this report, and the window used is shown at the top.

## Money
Reports › Money shows stock value, cost of goods, food cost % against your target, count history and exports. Food cost appears once you have sales and at least two counts.

## Exports
Products, Count, Variance, Reorder and Report CSVs download from the program for spreadsheets or your bookkeeper.
