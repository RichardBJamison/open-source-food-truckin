## Trucks
On the truck › Trucks. **Add truck** to run more than one; each truck has its own products, counts and logs. **Switch** changes which truck you are working on. You can copy products from another truck to start a new one quickly.

## Transfers
**Transfer** moves stock from this truck to another. You need at least two trucks. Transfers show in the Input log.

## Crew board
On the truck › Board holds notes for the crew. Post a note, pin the important ones to the top, and mark a note for this truck only.
