## Backups
Settings › Data. A backup is saved automatically before every reset or restore. Press **Back up now** any time, and **Download backup** to keep a copy off this computer (a USB stick or your cloud drive). Your AI key is never put in a backup.

## Restore
Upload a backup .zip or .json you downloaded before and press **Restore**. It replaces the current data, so a backup is made first.

## Sample data
**Load sample taco truck** fills this truck with demo products and counts, all marked EXAMPLE. It replaces this truck's products and counts (a backup is made first).

## Clearing data
- **Clear this truck's data** removes products, counts and logs for this truck only.
- **Erase everything** removes every truck, person and setting. Type RESET to confirm.

## Where the data lives
Everything is in the program's `data` folder on this computer: `store.json`, `config.json` and `photos`. Copy that folder to move to a new computer.
