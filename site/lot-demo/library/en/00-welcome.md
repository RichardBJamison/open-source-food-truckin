## What this program is
Open Source Food Truckin' (OSFT) is free inventory for food trucks. It runs on your own computer, needs no account, and works in English and Spanish. You map your truck once, count it by voice or by tapping, and the program tells you what you used, what it cost and what to buy before the next service.

Nothing leaves your computer unless you switch on the optional AI and press an AI button.

## The screens at a glance
The menu on the left has one button per job:
- **Home base**: today's numbers and quick paths: count, voice count, order list, load list.
- **Inventory sheets**: Count sheet, Voice count, Variance, Order list, Orders, Product master and Starter list.
- **Reports**: Sales vs counts (usage in dollars) and Money (stock value, food cost %, count history, exports).
- **On the truck**: Zones (the storage map), Trucks and the crew Board.
- **All inputs**: Input log, POS sales, Invoices & receiving, and Invoice (bulk lines).
- **Menu & costing**: plates, recipes, cost per plate and food cost %.
- **Stops & load lists**: PAR per stop type and a printable load list.
- **Settings**: Business, Sections, Count types, AI, People, Vendors, Accounts, Data and this **Library**.

## How to get help
- **Library** (Settings › Library) is this manual. Search any word, or follow a path.
- **Help** is the round button in the bottom-right corner of every screen. Ask it anything in plain words. Without AI it answers straight from the Library. If you connect your own AI in Settings › AI, it becomes a tutor that walks you through the steps and can look at your truck's numbers (it reads them, it never changes them).
- Every Library page has a **Take me there** button that opens the screen it talks about.

## English and Spanish
Tap **EN** or **ES** at the top of the screen. Every screen, the Library and Help switch together, and the program remembers your choice on this device. Voice counting listens in the language you picked.

## Your first hour
1. Run the guided setup (it starts on its own the first time): name your truck, name your sections, walk them by voice.
2. Add product invoices during setup if you have them, then reconcile and approve your map.
3. Do your first count.
4. Import last night's POS sales and add your menu plates.
5. Count again at the end of the week and open Reports.
