## Open mode
Until you add the first admin with a PIN, the program is in open mode: anyone who can reach it gets in. Add an admin to lock it.

## Roles
- **Admin** does everything.
- **Manager** runs a shift: counts, inputs, reports, sheets, menu, orders, stops and the board, but not settings or people.
- **Crew** counts: home, counts, voice counts, the truck screens, stops and the board.

Each person also has a list of permissions you can tick on or off, a truck lock (keep them on one truck), and the count types they may start.

## Add someone
Settings › People › **Add person**: name, login name, role, truck and a 6-digit PIN typed twice.

## Logging in and out
People pick their name and enter their PIN. **Log out** is at the bottom of the menu.

## Forgotten PIN
An admin can press **Reset PIN** for anyone. There is no reset if the only admin forgets their own PIN, so keep a second admin.
