The Manifesto

Counting the cooler shouldn’t
cost you a festival’s tips.

This is the honest version of why Open Source Food Truckin' exists, who it’s for, and why the whole program — cooler, freezer, prep, and load list included — is free and stays free.

The Bill Nobody Talks About

Inventory has been a paid problem for decades.

Somewhere along the way, an industry decided that counting your own product was worth charging you for. Trucks inherited restaurant invoices.

The Platform

$249+ / mo

Restaurant inventory SaaS (MarketMan Starter published around $249/mo in 2026) built for four walls, billed every month, forever.

The POS add-on

Quote

Toast / xtraCHEF-class invoice tools that want your POS first and a sales call second. Trucks already pay for the tablet.

The Spreadsheet

$0, until it isn’t

A sheet that worked for twelve SKUs and broke the week you added a second stop type.

Promises we actually keep

  • No investors to answer to, so no pressure to add a paywall later.
  • No email capture on the download — the program does not need your address to work.
  • No seat limits, no location tax, no “upgrade to Pro” hiding a count type you already need.
  • Open source, so if we ever drifted from this, anyone could see it in the code and fork it.

A Richard B. Jamison project. Sister to Open Source Barware. Free forever.
