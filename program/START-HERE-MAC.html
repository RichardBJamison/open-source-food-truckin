Open Source Food Truckin' — Mac install

Free, local, yours. About two minutes.

The short version: double-click Install.command in this folder. A Terminal window sets everything up and opens the program in your browser at http://127.0.0.1:5053/. Bookmark that page — that bookmark is the software.

If your Mac says it “can't be opened” or “Apple could not verify” it

That's normal for free software downloaded from a website (we're not an App Store app). Do this once:

  1. Click Done (or Cancel) on the warning.
  2. Open System Settings → Privacy & Security, scroll down, and click Open Anyway next to “Install.command was blocked”.
  3. Double-click Install.command again and click Open Anyway / Open.

Older macOS (14 and earlier): right-click Install.command → Open → Open.

Or skip the warning entirely (Terminal)

  1. Open Terminal (Applications → Utilities).
  2. Type bash (with a space), drag Install.command from this folder into the window, press Return.

If it asks for “command line developer tools”

Your Mac is offering to install Python. Click Install, wait for it to finish, then run Install.command again.

After install

  • The program lives in your home folder: osft-program. You can delete this download folder.
  • It starts by itself when you log in. Start.command / Stop.command are in osft-program.
  • Updating: download the new version and run its Install.command. Your data stays.
  • Phone on the same Wi-Fi: see README-INSTALL.txt.

Instalación en Mac

Haz doble clic en Install.command. Se abre el programa en http://127.0.0.1:5053/. Guarda esa página en favoritos: ese favorito es el software.

Si tu Mac dice que no se puede abrir

  1. Haz clic en OK / Cancelar.
  2. Abre Ajustes del Sistema → Privacidad y seguridad y haz clic en Abrir igualmente.
  3. Vuelve a hacer doble clic en Install.command y elige Abrir.

O en Terminal: escribe bash , arrastra Install.command a la ventana y pulsa Return.

Si pide instalar “herramientas de desarrollo”, haz clic en Instalar y luego corre Install.command otra vez.
