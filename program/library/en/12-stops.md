## Stop types
A lunch lot isn't a festival. Stops & load lists › **Add stop type** (Lunch, Brewery, Festival…) and set a **PAR for this stop** on the products that change. Leave a PAR blank to use the product's everyday PAR.

## Tomorrow's load list
Pick **Tomorrow's stop** and the date. The list compares what's on hand to that stop's PAR and shows what to load and what to buy or prep, with the number of products short. Press **Printable load list** the night before.

## Good to know
The load list assumes what's on hand is on the truck. If you keep stock at a commissary, set it up as a second truck and move stock with a transfer.
