## Speak your inventory
Inventory sheets › Voice count. Tap **Start talking** and say a section, then products and amounts: "cooler, chicken 3 cases, crema 2". Tap **Stop**, check the text (you can edit it) and press **Check what I said**.

## How to speak it
- One product at a time, amount after the name: "cheese 6, sour cream 2 tubs".
- Say the unit if it isn't the product's normal unit. "Half" and "and a half" work.
- English, Spanish or a mix are all fine; the mic listens in the language you picked at the top.

## Check what we heard
Each line is marked:
- **Matched**: found in your list.
- **Which one?**: more than one product fits; pick the right one.
- **Not found**: pick the product or ignore the line.
- **Different unit**: you said a unit that isn't the product's unit; check the amount.

**Not said** lists products you didn't mention: keep their last count or set them to 0. Big jumps from the last count are flagged so you can double-check them.

When everything is right, press **Save these counts** or **Save & see variance**.

## Which browsers can listen
Chrome, Edge and Safari. Firefox can't listen, so it shows the typing box only. Typing always works everywhere.
