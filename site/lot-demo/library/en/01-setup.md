## How setup works
Setup is a walk-through of your truck in two passes. You talk (or type), the program writes it down, and you check it. Nothing is saved until you approve each step.

The steps are: Welcome (optional AI) › Your truck › Sections › Walk › Reconcile › Approve map › First count › Lock (optional).

## Step 1: your truck
Give the truck a name and pick how often you count (weekly, every 2 weeks or monthly). The name shows on orders and reports.

## Step 2: sections
Sections are the places you keep things: cooler, lowboy, dry shelf, cab, window. Add, rename, delete and reorder them so they match your truck. Put them in the order you walk the truck: counts follow this order. Tap a suggestion to add a common food-truck section.

## Pass 1: walk and map
Go section by section. Tap the mic and say what is there, product then amount: "ground beef 14 pounds, chicken thigh 20 pounds, shredded cheese 6 bags". Commas or pauses between products are fine. If you don't know a count, just say the name; you will count in Pass 2. You can type instead of talking.

After each section, check what was heard. Untick anything wrong, fix names and amounts, then add it to the section.

With AI on you can also press **Photo › AI** to suggest a section's products from a shelf photo, or **Tidy with AI** to clean up a messy spoken list. AI only suggests; you confirm.

## Invoices during setup
In the same step you can add recent product invoices: PDFs, photos, CSV files or typed lines like "2 cs Ground beef 4.89". The program matches them to what you said and carries over pack size, unit, cost and vendor. Without AI, CSV files and PDFs with real text are read as best effort; photos and scanned PDFs need AI, or type the lines.

## Reconcile
Reconcile merges what you said with your invoices. For each section you see "Here's what I registered": the count you spoke, whether it is on an invoice, the cost and a status. Products on an invoice but not in any section are listed so you can put them in a section, make a new section, mark them "Same as" another product, commissary only, not stocked on the truck, or skip.

Tap any row to edit it: name, section, count, unit, pack, cost, vendor, delete, merge, or link and unlink an invoice line. If you said one unit and the invoice says another, the row is flagged so you can pick the right one. Changes show right away but are saved only when you press **Confirm & continue**.

## Approve the map and first count
Check every product section by section, set PAR (how much you want on hand), then **Approve map**. Pass 2 is your first count: count each section by voice or by typing, check the numbers and save. That count becomes the starting point for usage and reorders.

## Lock it (optional)
Add an owner PIN so staff only see what you allow. Skip it if it's just you; you can add people later in Settings › People.

## Run setup again
Settings › Business has **Run setup again**. Your products stay.
